In many cultures, gods are born gods.
They come from heaven, mythology, or ancient creation stories.
But in China, something unusual can happen.
A human being can die…
and centuries later,
people may worship that person as a god.
How?
In traditional Chinese belief,
the boundary between humans and gods is not absolute.
A person can start as ordinary,
then become a hero,
a symbol,
and eventually a divine figure.
But becoming a god is not simply a reward for being powerful.
It is about what that person represents.
Loyalty.
Justice.
Protection.
Wisdom.
When generations of people continue to believe that someone represents these values,
their memory slowly transforms into something more.
Their story becomes a legend.
Their legend becomes a belief.
And their belief becomes a god.
In China, gods are not always born in heaven.
Sometimes, they are created by human memory.
