Why do Chinese zombies drink human blood?
Unlike Western vampires,
Chinese zombies are not driven by hunger or desire.
The answer comes from an ancient Chinese idea:
balance.
Ancient Chinese believed that everything in the world contained two opposite forces:
Yin and Yang.
Yin represented darkness, coldness, and stillness.
Yang represented warmth, life, and movement.
A living person existed in balance between these two forces.
But according to Chinese folklore,
a Jiangshi was created when this balance was destroyed.
A corpse buried under unusual conditions,
surrounded by too much Yin energy,
could slowly transform into a creature trapped between life and death.
But this transformation came with a problem.
The Jiangshi's body was no longer balanced.
It was filled with darkness and lacked the energy of life.
So it searched for the strongest source of Yang energy:
the blood of the living.
Because in ancient Chinese belief,
blood represented the power of life itself.
That is why Chinese zombies drink blood.
Not because they are hungry.
But because they are trying to recover the balance they lost.
A Western vampire seeks immortality.
A Chinese Jiangshi seeks something different:
a way back to balance between life and death.
