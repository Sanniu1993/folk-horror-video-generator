Most people think Chinese zombies are just the Chinese version of the undead.
But in ancient Chinese legends, the birth of a zombie comes from a completely different belief.
Ancient people believed that the human body was not just a physical object.
It was connected to the energy of the natural world.
If a corpse was buried in a special place,
something unusual could happen.
A place with no sunlight,
but exposed to moonlight.
A place where dark energy gathered.
A location with unusual terrain and hidden forces.
Over many years,
the corpse could absorb the energy of its surroundings,
and slowly transform.
Eventually,
it could become a creature between life and death.
A Jiangshi.
Unlike Western zombies,
Chinese zombies are not created by viruses.
They are not driven by hunger or the desire to destroy humans.
They are created when the natural order of death is disturbed.
Because in traditional Chinese belief,
death is not the end of a person's connection with nature.
Even after death,
the relationship between humans and the world continues.
